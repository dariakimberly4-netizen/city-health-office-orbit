# City Health Office Orbit

Orbit-first mobile-friendly City Health Office operations app.

## Opening screen
The circular City Health Office Orbit is the first application screen.

## Modules
- Home-Visit Planner
- Referral Tracking
- Immunization Follow-Up
- Senior & PWD Monitoring
- Medicine Distribution Log
- Emergency Alerts
- City Health Reports
- Today's Local Care Activity
- Clickable City Health Command Center

All modules open functional workspaces and can save browser-local records. Supabase/backend integration is intentionally deferred for a later phase.
